## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the commanding presence, discretion, and timeless prestige of elite corporate advisory, private wealth management, and senior leadership consultancies. Inspired by high-end private architectural chambers—paneled walls, brushed brass mouldings, polished stone surfaces, and bespoke tailoring—the interface communicates authority, discretion, and unshakeable confidence.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Luxury**:
- Generous, measured negative space paired with razor-sharp editorial restraint.
- Architectural framing using micro-borders in burnished metallic tones instead of aggressive flat dividers.
- High-contrast visual cadence between profound deep midnight blue and warm, luminous alabaster parchment.
- Measured, restrained interactions that feel deliberate, weighted, and substantial rather than frivolous or hyperactive.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in heritage corporate navy and illuminated by warm burnished gold accents against an ivory and cream foundation.

- **Primary (`#0B192C`)**: Deep midnight sovereign navy. Used for prominent typography, primary button fills, key structural containers, and dominant navigation frames.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`)**: Burnished antique gold. Utilized as a prestige accent for badges, active states, delicate horizontal rules, focus indicators, and bespoke numerical callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#0F2038`)**: Regal night-blue surface tone. Functions as a secondary layer for card backdrops in dark mode or deep container states, providing nuanced tonal separation from the primary midnight.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm ivory cream. Serves as the default canvas surface, avoiding the sterile clinical glare of pure `#FFFFFF` and establishing an archival, tactile paper quality.
- **Neutral Accent Tones**: `#F8F5EE` for sub-layer cards and inset wells; `#E8E2D5` for muted borders and delicate dividers; `#4A5568` for secondary body typography.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances classical editorial mastery with utilitarian modern clarity. 

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Used exclusively for prominent titles, executive bios, section openers, and key data statements. It evokes legacy broadsheets, classical diplomas, and bespoke publications. Titles should be configured with snug letter-spacing to emphasize editorial weight.
- **Body & Labels (`Inter`)**: Used for analytical reports, metadata, navigation, data tables, and interactive controls. Its neutral, high-legibility geometry offsets the expressive flourish of the serif headers.
- **Uppercase Labels**: All `label-lg`, `label-md`, and `label-sm` tokens are deployed in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury watch dial and architectural plate engraving conventions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a **12-column fluid grid** for desktop, collapsing to an **8-column grid** on tablets and a **4-column grid** on mobile.

- **Cadence & Density**: Executive interfaces require room to breathe. Density is kept low-to-medium; components rely on deliberate whitespace rather than cramped partitions to articulate hierarchy.
- **Section Margin**: Generous `3rem` margins frame the viewport on desktop like a framed portrait, while mobile scales down to `1.25rem` to preserve precious horizontal scan areas.
- **Rhythm**: Element-to-element relationships adhere strictly to the 4px base increment (`space-xs` through `space-xl`), ensuring structural consistency from executive dashboards to comprehensive corporate profiles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast metallic architectural framing** and **diffused ambient illumination** rather than stacked drop shadows.

- **Hairline Framing**: Structural depth is primarily articulated through fine `1px` borders in `#E8E2D5` (on light surfaces) or `#C5A059` at `25%` opacity (on midnight navy containers).
- **Ambient Floor Shadows**: When floating dialogs or elevated executive dossier cards require separation, apply ultra-soft, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with navy:
  - *Standard Card Elevation*: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`
  - *Overlay / Executive Modal Elevation*: `0 16px 40px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.12)`
- **Sconce Glow Effect**: For high-emphasis cards or heroic partner portraits, a delicate top-edge internal highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.3)`) mimics directional sconce lighting on architectural woodwork.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** posture:
- Standard UI elements (inputs, standard buttons, tabs, tags) carry a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, containers, and modal dialogs utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Large showcase canvases utilize up to `0.75rem` (12px).

Rounded pill shapes and hyper-circular buttons are avoided entirely. The geometry must feel tailored, architectural, and razor-sharp, maintaining the precision of bespoke boardroom joinery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0B192C` fill, `#FDFBF7` text, `4px` radius, styled with an inset border of `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.4)`. Hover state transitions to `#0F2038` with an expanded gold border opacity.
- **Secondary / Prestige**: Solid `#FDFBF7` surface, `1px solid #C5A059` border, `#0B192C` text. Hover fills with `#F8F5EE` and crisp gold text.
- **Ghost**: Transparent fill, `#0B192C` text, paired with an underline accent rendered in `#C5A059`.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed within an ivory-grounded field (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E8E2D5` border.
- Active/Focused state eliminates heavy glow rings; instead, it renders a crisp `1px solid #C5A059` boundary accompanied by a gentle transition of the floating label to `#C5A059`.

### Cards & Executive Panels
- Constructed using `#F8F5EE` canvas fills bordered by `#E8E2D5`.
- Header areas feature `headline-sm` in `Playfair Display` followed by a delicate horizontal divider line (`#E8E2D5`).
- Dark-themed featured dossier cards use `#0B192C` backgrounds with `#C5A059` header text and `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` dividers.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact rectangular forms with `4px` corner radii.
- Styled with a pale parchment background (`#F8F5EE`), subtle gold border (`#C5A059` at `40%`), and uppercase label tracking (`label-sm`) in `#0B192C`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square checkbox (`4px` radius) and circular radio elements framed in `1.5px solid #0B192C`.
- Selected states fill with `#0B192C` and feature a centered tick or dot in `#C5A059`.

### Executive Dossier & Metric Callouts
- Metrics feature oversized numerals rendered in `Playfair Display` with a `#C5A059` burnished gold tint, anchored underneath by uppercase metadata labels (`label-md`) in `#4A5568`.
