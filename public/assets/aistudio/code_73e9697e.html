The Foundation Care Dossier

In times of sudden change, frantic searching through drawers and disconnected patient portals creates dangerous friction. The Foundation Care Dossier establishes a singular, authoritative baseline that travels with the client to emergency chambers, new specialists, and family conferences.

Master Physician Index

Complete listing of attending physicians, clinic lines, and specialty notes.

Emergency Escalation Protocol

Pre-arranged proxies, hospital preferences, and power of attorney directives.

Observational Health History

Surgical milestones, allergies, vital baseline norms, and chronic factors.

Daily Living Rhythm Guide

Personal routines, mobility precautions, dietary regimens, and preferences.

Strictly Non-Clinical Scope of Service Notice: Southern Well Senior Concierge provides 100% non-clinical care navigation, logistical oversight, physician appointment advocacy, and strategic coordination for families throughout South Mississippi.
